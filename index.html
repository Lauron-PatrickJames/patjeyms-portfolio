Letter to Stakeholders

02Page

To friends, collaborators, and recruiters who scrolled this far:

My name is Patrick, but most people call me Pat. I'm an Information Technology student at De La Salle University – Dasmariñas on the Web Development track, and this was a busy year. I build full-stack web apps, and lately I've moved closer to the model side of things as a backend AI engineer at FlyRank AI, benchmarking LLMs and retrieval techniques for AI search and ranking.

My biggest build this year is AniSkolar, the scholarship management system I'm making for my university as my capstone. It takes paperwork that used to live in folders and spreadsheets and turns it into dashboards, automated reports, and a sign-in that just uses your school account.

Away from the code editor I edit video, take photos, and design graphics. That started as freelance work and carried on when I co-directed my college student government's media committee. I also serve there as a legislative councilor, drafting and passing ordinances for students in my college.

The outlook for the coming year is strong. I graduate in 2027, and I'm always glad to hear about interesting work in web and AI engineering.

patjeyms Patrick James S. Lauron, BS Information Technology, Class of 2027

Selected Work, 2025 – 2026

01

AniSkolar

Undergraduate capstone · Team of 3 · May 2026 – Present

The scholarship portal for De La Salle University – Dasmariñas, used by the Admissions and Scholarship Office and 14 other offices. Students apply, renew and log duty hours; offices review applications, run renewals with automatic eligibility checks, and post announcements to the portal, Facebook and Outlook. I built the frontend, the Express and MongoDB API, and the deployment.

Travelogue

Personal project · Dec 2025

A travel planning companion that puts flight search, hotel booking, and itinerary planning in one app. It pulls live flight prices and hotel data from SerpAPI and TripAdvisor, maps your itinerary and nearby landmarks with Leaflet, and follows WCAG 2.1 with print-friendly booking confirmations.
