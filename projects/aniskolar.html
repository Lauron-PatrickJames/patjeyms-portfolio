Background

Statement of the Problem

A2Page

Scholarships at DLSU-D aren't run by one office. The Admissions and Scholarship Office handles the Student Financial Aid Grant (SFAG), Entrance and EBPSP scholarships, while POLCA, the Alumni office, the Sports Development Office, ROTC, the student publications, the University Student Government and seven college student governments each run their own, with their own forms, rules and deadlines.

Being a scholar is also ongoing, not a one-time application. SFAG scholars render duty hours in campus offices every semester, office heads evaluate their work, and every scholar renews each semester with their grades. Deciding a renewal means cross-checking GPA, failing grades, documents, logged hours and an evaluation, for every scholar, every semester.

AniSkolar puts that whole lifecycle in one place: finding a scholarship, applying, being reviewed, logging duty, being evaluated and renewing. It also enforces the offices' rules, so staff stop checking them by hand, and keeps each office's data separate from every other office's.

Who it serves

Four Divisions of Users

A3Page

Students

Microsoft school sign-in

Explore and compare scholarships with eligibility warnings, apply with document checklists and auto-saved drafts, track every application on a timeline, renew each semester, log duty hours and message the office.

Scholarship Office

The AdSO

Reviews applications with saved reply templates and one-click ZIP downloads, runs renewals with automatic eligibility checks, assigns duty offices, posts announcements to the portal, Facebook and Outlook, and exports statistics and the FSE report.

Other Offices

14 offices

Run their own scholarships end to end (open, close, decide, renew) and record their own criteria, such as ROTC ranks, publication points or a varsity coach's endorsement. AniSkolar turns those into a result.

Duty Office Heads

Signed in by office email

Confirm or flag the duties scholars log, and evaluate each scholar on a 1–5 form once their hours are complete. That evaluation feeds straight into the scholar's next renewal.

Decisions & trade-offs

Notes to the Case Study

A6Page
Note 1

The office's rules live on the server

Scholarship rules used to depend on someone remembering them. Now the API checks them whenever something is saved, so they can't be skipped by editing the page:

  • a student holds only one internally funded scholarship at a time (they can be approved for two and choose which to keep)
  • new SFAG applications are only accepted in June and July
  • POLCA takes 2 scholars per college; Alumni takes 2 for the whole campus
  • duty can only be logged Monday to Friday in office hours, never over the lunch break, at least 1.5 hours long, in 30-minute steps
Note 2

Renewals sort themselves

Every submitted renewal is checked automatically against the minimum GPA, failing grades, required documents, last semester's duty hours and the office head's evaluation. It's marked Eligible, Waiting, Needs review or Not eligible, with the reasons listed. Everyone who passes can be renewed with one button, so staff spend their time only on the cases that actually need a decision.

Note 3

Fifteen offices, one database, no leaks

Each office sees only its own applications, renewals and documents. A file can only be opened by the student who uploaded it or the office handling it. The server always identifies the student from the signed-in session, never from anything the browser sends, so one student can't ask for another student's data.

The same care went into messaging. Messages containing offensive words in English or Filipino, including ones disguised with symbols or extra letters, are flagged as you type and rejected by the server.

Note 4

Moving every file without changing a single record

Documents started out in MongoDB's GridFS, which was quickly filling the free database tier. I moved storage to a private Azure Blob container while keeping the same file IDs, so no application or renewal record had to change. Reads fall back to GridFS for anything not moved yet. The migration script ran as a dry run first, then copied each existing file and checked it byte-for-byte before deleting the original.

Note 5

Email that lands in Outlook, and keeps landing

Every student and staff mailbox at DLSU-D is Outlook, so email goes through Azure Communication Services. Mail waits in an outbox and goes out in small batches to stay within the service's limits. Failed sends are retried later, and anything that still fails is listed for the office to see. Message alerts jump the queue ahead of reminders, and switching email providers means changing one file.

Note 6

Hosting that stays awake

Scheduled announcements, the email outbox and the automated reminders all run on timers inside the API. On a free hosting plan the API went to sleep after 15 minutes idle, and in November 2026 it once failed to wake up. It now runs on an always-on instance in Singapore, in the same region as the database and the Azure services, to keep every request short.

Note 7

Tested like real people depend on it

210 API tests cover the rules above: approval chains, batch renewals, slot limits, rate limits and document ZIPs. 40 Playwright tests click through the app as a student, an AdSO admin and an office account. For demos there's a separate database, seeded with sample students and applications in every status, so a demo never touches real student data.
